The show

Fine Tuned Lights is a family-built roofline light show in northwest Oklahoma City, in its first season in 2026. Every bulb on the roof is its own pixel, so the whole house moves to the music. The music plays on your phone through the Tune In bar, so the street stays quiet.

The roof

Thirteen strands trace every eave, ridge, hip and dormer, the tower and the entry arch: about 700 pixels, one every six inches, each one addressed on its own. The strands are built long at the kitchen table and trimmed to fit on the roof on install day. The far right end of the house faces the back yard, so it stays dark on purpose.

The family

This is a family project. The kids help build the strands, choreograph the sequences and run the shows, and they have opinions about the songs.
